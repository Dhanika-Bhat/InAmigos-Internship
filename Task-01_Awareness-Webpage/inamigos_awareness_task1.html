Who We Are

InAmigos Foundation was founded on September 23, 2020 by Mr. Govind Shukla. In a world that feels divided, InAmigos stands as a beacon of unity, dedicated to uplifting underserved communities through compassion, education, and collective action.

Section 8 NGO Govt. Licensed NGO Darpan NITI Aayog ISO 9001:2015

Our Story

Building a More Compassionate India

Based in Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh, InAmigos Foundation has grown from a local initiative into a pan-India movement touching lives across 28+ states. Our work spans food security, quality education for underprivileged children, skill development for women and youth, animal welfare, and environmental protection.

With a strong network of dedicated professionals, corporate partners, and over 200 volunteers, we work tirelessly to bridge the gap between privilege and need — one act of kindness at a time.

Ongoing Initiatives

Our Six Core Projects

🍱

01 / PROJECT SEVA

Feeding Hope, Clothing Dignity

Project SEVA is our flagship humanitarian initiative — distributing food to the hungry and clothing to those in need. Our volunteers run regular food drives and clothing campaigns, ensuring that no one in our community goes without basic necessities. A small act of kindness can bring comfort, confidence, and a smile.

Food & Clothing
📚

02 / BACHPANSHALA

Nurturing Young Minds

Quality education for every underprivileged child. We create spaces where happiness meets learning, and every young mind is empowered with knowledge and hope for a brighter future.

Education
🦋

03 / PROJECT UDAAN

Soaring Towards Independence

Women empowerment through skill development, vocational training, and financial independence programs. We believe women are the pillars of a thriving society.

Women Empowerment
🌿

04 / PRAKRITI

Plant for a Better Tomorrow

Planting saplings with love and purpose — healing and protecting our Mother Earth, one tree at a time. Environmental conservation and sustainability for future generations.

Environment
🐾

05 / PROJECT JEEV

Empowering Lives, Spreading Compassion

Animal welfare through rescue, protection, feeding, and rehabilitation. Because every living being deserves kindness and care.

Animal Welfare

06 / PROJECT VIKAS

Enhancing Employability

Skill development and professional training for India's youth, with over 30,000 interns trained under this initiative — building careers and creating futures.

Skill Development
